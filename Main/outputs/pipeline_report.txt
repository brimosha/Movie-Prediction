====================================================================================================
3. CROSS-VALIDATION DESIGN (fixes the 'CV on the entire set' problem)
====================================================================================================
  * CV is run on the TRAIN file only. The test file is touched exactly once, at the very end.
  * Target transform, tuning, scaling and any thresholds are fitted inside the training part of each fold.
  * 1 x 3-fold Repeated STRATIFIED CV (stratified on 10 quantile bins of log gross), seed=42.
  * Hyper-parameter tuning for 'HistGB (nested-tuned)' happens in an INNER 3-fold loop within each outer training fold (nested CV), so its score is not optimistically biased.
  * Model selection uses mean CV RMSE on the Yeo-Johnson scale; test data play no role.
  * Total outer folds: 3; train/validation sizes ~ 2199/1100.
